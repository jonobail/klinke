## Signal flow (as built)

`send → BASS (low shelf 70 Hz) → TREBLE (high shelf 7 kHz) → (+) → soft clip → 13 kHz 4-pole LPF →
DelayNode → 13 kHz 4-pole LPF → FEEDBACK back to (+), and → passive LPF → ret`. MIXING is the base
class's equal-power dry / wet. A triangle oscillator modulates the delay time.

- **Delay time** = stages / (2 × clock). The DELAY knob sweeps the clock from 82 kHz (SHORT) to
  27.3 kHz (LONG); each RANGE button picks the tap (512, 4096, 8192, 12288, 16384 stages). That gives
  3.1–9.4 ms, 25–75, 50–150, 75–225, 100–300 ms, matching the specification and Table-3.
- **Passive delay output filter**: modelled as a two-section RC ladder → one biquad (exact second-order
  denominator). The series resistance isn't readable, so it's fitted to Table-1: R = 1.4 kΩ gives
  −2.5 dB (150 ms) and −3.3 dB (225 / 300 ms) at 3 kHz re 700 Hz (manual: −2.5, −3.1, −4.0 ±2 dB).
- **Modulation**: triangle 0.5–10 Hz; DEPTH full scale = 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 % peak-to-peak of the
  delay for the five ranges.

## Interpretation

- The DELAY knob's law (clock VCO response) isn't printed: exponential clock between the two limits.
- Depth for the 75 / 150 / 225 ms ranges is interpolated between the two printed end points.
- A BBD clock never runs backwards, so the modulation is capped where the delay would change faster
  than 0.9 s/s (only matters at high rate × depth on long ranges).
- The NE570 compander and pre / de-emphasis are omitted (they hide BBD noise); the loop's overload
  is a unity-gain tanh, letting FEEDBACK reach 1.05 for slow runaway.
- FOOT SW (kills the delay input) and the 9-LED input meter are not modelled; the display shows the
  delay time and range, which the hardware doesn't have.

## Roadmap

- Model the compander's breathing and the BBD's clock-noise / aliasing at long settings.
- FOOT SW as a rocker (delay input kill, repeats finish).
