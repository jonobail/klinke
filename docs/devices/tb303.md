# TB-303

Bass line synthesizer with a 16-step pattern sequencer. Source: _TB-303 Service Notes_, first
edition Feb. 1982, 3rd printing Sep. 1986, with the errata of 1992
(seriescircuits.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Roland-TB-303-Service-Manual.pdf): specifications
and top view p.1, block diagram p.3, main board circuit p.5 (VCO, VCF, ENV, VCA, accent, slide),
adjustment pp.6–7 (CV, VCO, VCF trims), VCF envelope modulation p.8.

Code: `core/tb303.ts` (maths, pattern logic and the catalog entry, tested in
`tests/tb303.test.ts`), `audio/tb303-unit.ts`, and its own panel `gear/tb303-panel.*`, laid out
like the silver original: the back-edge jacks; WAVEFORM and TUNING, CUT OFF FREQ, RESONANCE,
ENV MOD, DECAY, ACCENT along the top; tempo, pattern 1–8, the logo and VOLUME; a 16-step display;
and the dark switch board with RUN/STOP, PITCH MODE / TIME MODE, the C–C′ keys, DOWN / UP /
ACCENT / SLIDE (NOTE / TIE / REST in time mode), BACK, NEXT, CLEAR and LAST STEP.

## Modelled

- VCO: sawtooth, or a square squared up from it (Q8, p.3/p.5), WAVEFORM switch. Pitch at 1 V/oct;
  the A key is 110 Hz (TUNE trim, p.5), so the pattern's low C is C2 and DOWN / UP give the three
  octaves C1–C4 (CV 1 V–4 V, pp.5–7). TUNING ±700 cents, a perfect fifth (spec, p.1).
- VCF: 4-pole transistor ladder (TR.C LADDER, p.3/p.5), CUT OFF FREQ, RESONANCE up to a ringing
  peak (the VCF trim on p.7 shows it ringing at a 2 ms period with CUT OFF centred, so the knob's
  centre is ≈ 500 Hz).
- ENV GEN: decay only, retriggered by each new note. DECAY 200 ms – 2.5 s to 10 % (p.5).
  ENV MOD deepens the sweep _and_ lowers the starting cutoff, as Q9 biases Q10 / Q11 (p.8).
- ACCENT: an accented note forces the shortest decay (ACC ON/OFF across the DECAY pot, p.3); the
  envelope × ACCENT level goes to the VCA (louder) and, through R46 47 kΩ into C13 1 µF (τ 47 ms,
  p.5), to the VCF: the accent sweep, which piles up over consecutive accents. RESONANCE is a
  dual pot (VR4, p.1/p.5) whose second gang feeds this sweep, so more resonance gives more "wow".
- SLIDE: an RC on the CV (C35 0.22 µF switched in by IC12, p.5), τ 22 ms. A slid note keeps the
  gate open into the next NOTE, which glides there without a new attack.
- VCA: its own envelope (ENV GEN Q31 / Q32, p.3), opened by the gate.
- Sequencer: 16 steps a pattern (spec: 1–16 steps), sixteenth notes of the song tempo, clamped to
  ♩ = 40–300 (spec). Each step has a key C–C′, DOWN / UP, ACCENT, SLIDE and a time: NOTE, TIE
  (holds the note before) or REST. LAST STEP sets the pattern length. Gates are half a step long.
  8 patterns, stored in the patch text (the hardware keeps them on its memory back-up battery);
  pattern 1 starts with a demo line. A pattern chosen while running starts at the end of the
  current one.
- Keys, MIDI and NOTE IN play the voice directly (last-note priority; a key pressed while another
  is held slides to it without a new attack). While the sequencer runs, a key transposes the
  pattern by its distance from C instead, like TRANSPOSE in track play. Sequenced notes also go
  out of NOTE OUT.

## Interpretation

- Each step carries its own pitch and time. On the hardware, PITCH MODE and TIME MODE fill two
  separate lists and the pitches are used up only by NOTE steps; one list per step reads better.
- Writing: PITCH MODE, then DOWN / UP / ACCENT / SLIDE as wanted and a key (or a note from the
  computer keyboard / MIDI, folded into the three octaves); the step under the cursor is written
  and the cursor moves on. TIME MODE: NOTE / TIE / REST on the DOWN / UP / ACCENT keys. Click a
  step to move the cursor; BACK / NEXT step it. CLEAR resets the pattern to 16 low Cs.
- The ladder is two cascaded 2-pole low-passes, the second with the resonance. The cutoff range
  (70 Hz – 3.5 kHz on the knob), the ENV MOD depth (up to 4 octaves, lowering the start by up to
  1¼ octaves), the accent sweep depth (up to 2 octaves) and the VCA envelope (3 ms attack, ~1.2 s
  decay, 6 ms release) are our choices; the notes give the circuit, not these figures.
- The 100 kΩ in the slide RC is our reading of the schematic.
- The TEMPO knob follows the song tempo; tracks, pattern groups, TAP / SYNC, MIX IN, CV / GATE
  and the headphone output aren't modelled. RUN/STOP is a panel action, not saved with the patch.
