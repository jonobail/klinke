# SH-101

Monophonic synthesizer with arpeggiator and sequencer. Source: *SH-101 Service Notes*, first edition,
Nov. 1982 (manuals.fdiskc.com): specifications and top view p.1, block diagram p.2, CPU program
pp.3–4 (keyboard read, clock check, random data, load / play / arpeggio).

Code: `core/sh101.ts` (maths, tested in `tests/sh101.test.ts`), `audio/sh101-unit.ts`, catalog entry
`sh101` in `core/gear.ts`; the panel uses the generic synth panel (sliders, selectors, REST button).

## Modelled

- VCO: sawtooth and a comparator-made pulse (saw + bias, squared), PULSE WIDTH 50 % → ~3 %, PWM from
  LFO / MAN / ENV; RANGE 16'–2'; TUNE ±50 cent; VCO MOD (vibrato) from the modulator.
- SUB OSC: −1 octave square, −2 octave square, −2 octave 25 % pulse; NOISE; SOURCE MIXER levels.
- VCF: 10 Hz – 20 kHz, RES to the edge of oscillation, ENV, MOD and KYBD (0–100 %) amounts.
- VCA: ENV or GATE. ENV: A 1.5 ms–4 s, D / R 2 ms–10 s, S; trigger GATE+TRIG (every key),
  GATE (legato, single trigger) or LFO (retriggers at the LFO rate while a key is held).
- MODULATOR: triangle, square, random (a new level on each clock tick) or noise; 0.1–30 Hz.
- Keys: 32 (F–C), last-note priority; PORTAMENTO OFF / ON / AUTO (legato only), 0–5 s;
  TRANSPOSE L / M / H; bender to VCO and VCF (B.VCO / B.VCF depths).
- ARPEGGIO UP / U&D / DOWN and SEQUENCER LOAD / PLAY, both clocked by the LFO rate, as on the
  hardware (§6 Clock Check). LOAD starts a new sequence; keys add notes, REST adds a rest, up to
  100 steps (spec). PLAY transposes the line by the key held. HOLD latches notes / the arp chord.
  The sequence is saved with the patch (the hardware keeps it on its memory back-up battery).

## Interpretation

- The 4-pole IR3109 filter is two cascaded 2-pole low-passes, resonance on the second.
- Arpeggio / sequencer gate length is half a clock period.
- Bender ranges (±1 octave VCO, ±4 octaves VCF at full depth) and the VCO MOD / VCF MOD depth
  curves are our choice; the service notes don't print them.
- The 'MOD' grip (LFO MOD) on the bender, EXT CLK, CV / GATE in and out jacks aren't modelled.
